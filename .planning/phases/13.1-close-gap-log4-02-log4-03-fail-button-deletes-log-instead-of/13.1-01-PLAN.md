<objective>
Fix the two `apply()` dispatch-type bugs where swiping "Fail" on a habit row — on both the Today view and the History view — calls `markUncompleted` (which DELETES the log row) instead of `markFailed` (which correctly writes `{status:'failed'}`). This closes the real gap behind LOG4-02 (Today) and LOG4-03 (History): both were marked complete by Phase 12's roadmap entry without this bug being caught, and REQUIREMENTS.md still marks them Pending. Per D-01/D-02, exactly two call sites change; three sibling `markUncompleted` call sites are structurally identical in shape but semantically correct and must NOT change.

Purpose: Since Phase 12 shipped (2026-09-01), every user who swiped "Fail" on a habit has had that day's log silently deleted instead of recorded as a conscious failure — indistinguishable from "never tracked", corrupting the failed-vs-undefined signal that S1/S2/S3 scoring and CSV export depend on (D-43).

Output: A regression test (`tests/integration/swipe-fail.test.js`) proving both call sites via a real DOM click through the real `apply()` chokepoint, two one-word production fixes, two stale-JSDoc corrections, and updated requirement-tracking docs.
</objective>

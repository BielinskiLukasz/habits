<objective>
Close the I18N-02 residual identified by the v1.2 milestone audit and independently confirmed by `13-VERIFICATION.md`: `js/views/settings/builders.js` hardcodes the English literal `'Undo last action'` at three sites inside `buildDataCard`'s Undo section — the enabled-branch button's `aria-label` (line 484), the disabled-branch button's `aria-label` (line 494), and the disabled-branch button's `text` (line 497) — instead of calling `t('settings.data.undoBtn')` like the enabled-branch button's `text` already does (line 486). Per D-01/D-02 (CONTEXT.md), all three sites become `t('settings.data.undoBtn')` calls; no new locale key is introduced (D-03) since the key already exists and is already translated in both `en.js` and `pl.js`.

Purpose: A Polish-locale user currently sees English text ("Undo last action") on the disabled undo-button's label and aria-label regardless of language preference, and the enabled button's aria-label has the same defect independent of its (already-correct) visible text — contradicting REQUIREMENTS.md's I18N-02 claim ("zero hardcoded UI strings remain across both shells") and D-44's documented i18n architecture. This residual escaped Phase 10's scan (which only checked `showErrorToast`/`showUndoToast` call sites, not raw builder object literals) and Phase 13's QA-01 review, and was explicitly out of Phase 13.1's scope (which was scoped only to LOG4-02/LOG4-03).

Output: Three one-line production fixes in `js/views/settings/builders.js`, plus a regression test in `tests/unit/builders.settings.test.js` that proves both the disabled button's `aria-label` and `text` track the active locale (asserted by switching to `'pl'`, where the correct translated output differs from the old hardcoded English literal) — closing D-04's requirement that this residual cannot silently reappear.
</objective>
